About

PhD Researcher & Software Developer

I’m Roohana, a Computer Science PhD researcher at Louisiana State University. My primary research examines datasets in computer security through systematic literature analysis, manual annotation, and verification.

I contributed to the extraction, manual labeling, and verification of 7,865 dataset instances. In a separate project, I built an LLM-based extraction pipeline and developed prompts to investigate how well models perform related information-extraction and labeling tasks. I also study model-specific patterns in generated code.

Outside my research, I build applications. My cooking journal, Savorly, combines React and TypeScript with an NVIDIA Nemotron-powered assistant.

Research

PRIMARY RESEARCH

Datasets in Computer Security

First-author research combining systematic dataset analysis with extensive manual annotation. I contributed to extracting, labeling, and verifying 7,865 dataset instances, and developed an evidence-based framework to help stakeholders assess dataset construction reporting.

  1. Extract dataset information
  2. Manually label instances
  3. Verify against source papers
  4. Analyze dataset practices

Related work: LLM-based extraction and labeling

In a separate project, I built a Python pipeline and developed prompts to extract and label research information with LLMs, investigating how well automated methods perform related tasks.

LLM authorship and source attribution

Ongoing research

Investigating model-specific coding behavior using artifacts generated by 10 locally hosted language models. Developing baselines with character n-gram features, XGBoost, and random forests.

Planned ablations examine temperature, top-p, and adversarial prompting under controlled conditions.
